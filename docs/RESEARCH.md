# User research plan

## Evidence used for version 0.1

The first product brief uses one direct user case:

- Long music sessions on a Mac
- A preference for wide and coherent sound
- Bluetooth headphone use
- Repeated audio popping in an unstable route
- Strong rejection of daemon restarts and hidden background behavior

The local route test adds technical evidence. It does not replace user
research.

## Next five interviews

Recruit five Mac listeners who use headphones for at least ten hours each
week. Include built-in, USB, and Bluetooth outputs.

Ask each person to complete the first-run acceptance path. Observe the person
before you explain a control. Record permission friction, activation time,
control confusion, perceived change at matched volume, and route confidence.

## Research controls

- Match loudness before sound comparisons.
- Do not describe the expected sound before the first comparison.
- Record negative reactions as carefully as positive reactions.
- Separate sound preference from route reliability.
- Do not collect music history or private audio.

## Evidence log

Add each interview as a dated repository discussion or private research note.
Convert repeated problems into issues. Link each product change to the evidence
that supports it.
